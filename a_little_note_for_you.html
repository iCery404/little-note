To you,

This is not a confession. I want to be very clear about that from the start. It is just a note, written on a very ordinary day, about a very ordinary person who keeps ending up in my thoughts. Nothing more. Probably.

I only wrote it because I noticed a few things, and noticing things is apparently what I do now. Like how a room feels a little different when you laugh. Or how I check my phone a bit more often than I need to, and absolutely not because of anyone in particular.

I am not saying anything. I am only saying that if I were to say something, hypothetically, it would sound a lot like this letter, just with many more nervous pauses.

So let us call it a secret that is not really a secret. A little sentence tucked between the lines, for you to find only if you are curious enough to look. If you find it, lovely. If you do not, then this was simply a kind note from someone who thinks you are easy to be around.

There is no pressure to decode anything. No answer needed. I would honestly rather keep things light, take it slowly, and see where one ordinary day leads to the next. I am not in a hurry, and I am very good at waiting.

But if you ever catch yourself smiling at this, even a little, and wondering what I might not be saying, maybe you have already figured out the part I left out. Maybe not. Either way, I will be around.

Anyway. Drink some water, get some rest, and be a little kind to yourself today. You do a lot more right than you give yourself credit for. That part, at least, I am saying plainly.

P.S. Read it again. Slower this time.

from someone who is definitely not confessing anything
